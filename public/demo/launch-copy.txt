Maker introduction
Hi Product Hunt! 👋

Parallax Print turns a flat image into printable pieces for a physical corner. From one chosen viewing position, the pieces line up as a single image. Move away, and the illusion breaks apart.

You can:

- Set the dimensions and angle of two walls, with an optional floor.
- Upload a PNG or JPEG and position the artwork in 3D.
- Orbit the installation, then select “Meet the eye level” to see the image align.
- Preview it against a reference photo using manually marked surfaces.
- Export tiled PDFs, calibration sheets, and an installation guide.

Artwork and project processing stay in your browser. Five editable layouts give you a starting point, from a simple right angle to a tight fold or a wall-to-floor wrap.

This first version is a digital alpha. I’d love feedback from artists, designers, educators, and anyone trying a small printed installation. Check your printed scale and measurements before mounting the pieces.
